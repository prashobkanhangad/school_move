Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 August 2026

These terms apply to the SchoolMove website and platform. SchoolMove is provided to schools for managing school transport operations and sharing journey status with parents and drivers.

Use of the platform

  • Schools are responsible for the accuracy of buses, routes, students, and parent links they configure.
  • Drivers should use the Driver App only for assigned trips and while they are authorised to operate the vehicle.
  • Parents may view transport status only for children the school has linked to their account.

Live location

Live location is collected from the driver device during an active trip so the school and linked parents can see bus progress. Location is used for transport operations, not advertising.

Accounts

Keep login credentials confidential. Schools control who has admin, driver, and parent access.

Contact

For account or data questions, contact your school’s transport administrator.
