Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

SchoolMove is a school transport management platform operated for schools, parents, and drivers. This page describes how the SchoolMove website and platform handle information.

What we collect

  • Account details used to sign in (name, email, role)
  • School transport data provided by the school (buses, routes, students, assignments)
  • Live bus location during an active trip, originating from the driver device
  • Transport notifications such as pickup, drop, delays, and emergencies

How it is used

Information is used to operate school transport: live tracking, ETAs, pickup/drop status, and operational alerts. We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads in the apps.

App-specific policies

  • Parent App privacy policy
  • Driver App privacy policy

Contact

For privacy requests about a student’s transport record, contact your school’s transport administrator. They control which students are linked to parent accounts.
